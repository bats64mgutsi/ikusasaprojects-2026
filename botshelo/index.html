what is to be homeless ?

To be homeless means not having a fixed,safe and stable place to sleep at night. It goes beyond just not having a roof -it means living in constant uncertainty, without a private space to rest,store belongings or feel secure. Homelessness can look like sleeping on the streets or somewhere else but it strips away basic dignity and makes everyday tasks like bathing,eating or staying healthy enormously difficult. More than anything, its a state of deep vulnerability , where a person is cut off from the stability and safety that most of us take for granted.

Causes of being homeless.

  • Lack of affordable housing
  • Poverty,inequality and unemployment
  • Inadequate social safety nets
  • Systemic discrimination
  • Conflict and forced displacement
  • Leaving institutional settings
  • Domestic abuse and family breakdown
  • Adverse childhood experiences
  • Eviction from your home
  • Mental health
  • Substance misuse
  • A sudden crisis or change
  • Incarceration history

How does homelessness affect our country?

  • Deforestation and habitat disruption : Encampments often lead to tree felling for firewood and shelter, damaging local flora and disturbing wildlife habitats.
  • Resource strain : Makeshift shelters often use non-biodegradable materials and contribute to localized pollution.
  • Extreme weather : With no indoor shelter, they face deadly heat or coldness which are severe risks.
  • Pollution : Already vulnerable to poor health, they have high exposure to air pollution and heat.
  • Water access : Finding clear water is constant struggle
  • Climate events : Disasters like floods or fires hit them hardest-they have fewer resources to evacuate, are often overlooked in planning and can be displaced for months.
